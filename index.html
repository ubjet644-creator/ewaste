01 / THE BIG PICTURETHE PROBLEM WITH E-WASTE

Out of sight
shouldn't mean
out of mind.

What happens when our electronics reach the end of their useful life?

Discarded phones, chargers, batteries and other electronics become e-waste. When handled carelessly, they can harm people and the environment. But many contain valuable materials that can be recovered responsibly.

Here's what we can do ↗
↗

Use it longer

Repair, reuse or donate working devices before replacing them.

01
♻

Keep it separate

Electronics and batteries don't belong in your regular household bin.

02
✳

Recycle responsibly

Choose an authorised e-waste collection point or recycler.

02 / LITTLE STEPS, BIG IMPACTYOUR ACTION PLAN

Ready to make
a difference?

You don't have to be an expert. Start with the unused electronics around you and take it one step at a time.

01⌕

Find it

Look for unused cables, old phones, chargers and other electronics at home.

02♡

Think twice

Could it be repaired or reused? If it works, consider donating it instead.

03↻

Recycle right

Erase personal data, then take items to an authorised e-waste recycler. Handle damaged batteries with care.

✳

A little reminder: Ask an adult for help with batteries, damaged devices and finding an authorised collection point.
